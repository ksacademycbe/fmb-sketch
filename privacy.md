# FMB Sketch Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 12 November 2026*

FMB Sketch does not collect, store or share any personal data.

**Files.** PDF files you open are read entirely on your device and are never uploaded. Saved sketches stay in the app's own storage on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

**Purchases.** The monthly subscription and lifetime unlock are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We do not receive your name, payment details or any other personal information.

**Analytics and tracking.** The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and makes no network requests.

**Children.** The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
